How to Print at Staples

  1. Click the Download PNG buttons above to save both front and back images.
  2. Go to staples.com/print or visit your local Staples print center.
  3. Select Business Cards → Upload Your Design.
  4. Upload the front PNG as Side 1 and back PNG as Side 2.
  5. Choose paper: 16pt Matte or Premium Glossy (ask for soft-touch laminate if available).
  6. Select quantity and checkout. Standard turnaround is same-day to 3 business days.

Tip: The PNG files include 0.125" bleed. Tell the print shop your trim size is 3.5" × 2" so they cut correctly. The PDF option auto-sets the page size.
