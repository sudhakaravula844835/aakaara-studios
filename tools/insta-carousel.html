Definition of ākāra
Shape. Form. The essence of what makes something visible — the moment an emotion takes physical form. From the ancient Sanskrit root, Aakaara is the art of giving shape to what you feel.
Mission of Aakaara
Cinematic storytelling rooted in South Indian heritage, crafted in New York City. Emotionally driven and visually intentional.
