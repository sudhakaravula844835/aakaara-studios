01 · Connect

Connect

We start with a conversation about your story, comfort level, and the kind of energy you want the session to hold. Sometimes that means a quick call. Sometimes it means mapping out your favorite part of the city before we ever pick up the camera.

02 · Shape

Shape the Vision

Outfits, locations, timing, weather, movement, and mood all matter. We plan just enough to make the session feel effortless while leaving room for spontaneity once it begins.

03 · Session

The Photoshoot

You don't need to know how to pose. Direction is gentle, intentional, and responsive to who you are together. The goal is never to force a performance — only to help you settle into the frame naturally.

04 · Edit

Edit & Storytelling

Color, contrast, black-and-white selections, pacing, and the order of the gallery are all shaped to feel cinematic and emotionally cohesive.

05 · Delivery

Delivery

Sneak peeks arrive quickly, and the final gallery is built to be revisited, shared, and printed — carrying the atmosphere of the day it was made.
